Dawrah Ilmiyah

دَوْرَةُ أَصْحَابِ الْهِمَّةِ الْعِلْمِيَّة

A three-day Islamic learning programme for youth and the wider Muslim community. Join us for beneficial study, reflection and reminders.

Dates: 1–3 December 20263 daysAfter ‘Asr to ‘Isha

Welcome to the Dawrah

We warmly invite young people and members of the wider community to register for the Dawrah Ilmiyah of As’haab Al Himma.

Books to be studied

The programme will cover the following works:

  • 1Al-Lāmiyyah – Ibn Taymiyyah رحمه الله
  • 2Shurūṭ al-Ṣalāh – Shaykh Muhammad ibn ‘Abd al-Wahhāb
  • 3Al-Ādāb al-‘Asharah – Shaykh al-‘Uṣaymī
  • 4Tafsīr al-Fātiḥah and Short Sūrahs
